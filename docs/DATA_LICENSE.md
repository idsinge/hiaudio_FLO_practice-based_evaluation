## Why this data is licensed differently

These files contain **pseudonymised respondent-level data**: one row per person, stable participant identifiers, and submission dates, gathered from nine members of a small, publicly named ensemble. They are not aggregate statistics and they are not fully anonymised.

Participants gave consent on terms that included confidentiality, use restricted to academic research, no sharing with third parties, and a right to request deletion of their data. An open licence permitting unrestricted redistribution would be inconsistent with those terms, so the response data is released under the more restrictive conditions below.
